# Taqbaylit · Taqbaylit (Latin script) — tizmilin n tsuqilt

Afaylu-agi yettaru tiseddarin anda yella kra i yesɛan assaɣ d tsuqilt: alugan i
yettwagezmen, awal yettwabeddlen, neɣ asteqsi i yettrajun ameẓẓuɣ n teqbaylit. Ameɣri
i yebɣan ad yessenqed taseddart yettwali ma yura-tt umsatal, ma tettwaṣeggem, neɣ ma
tbedd ɣef ulugan — u acimi.

Tizmilin ttwarunt s teglizit, axaṭer bdarent tajerrumt n taɛbranit, ilugan n wawalen
d yizrayen; teglizit d tutlayt n umahil yeddukklen gar tutlayin akk. Awalen n
teqbaylit d taɛbranit ttwarun akken llan.

Afaylu-agi yettnerni kan: tizmilin ttwaṣeggment, ur ttwakksent ara s tsusmi — amezruy
n Git d aḥric seg unagi.

---

## §1 · The Name — `Yahwe`

**Ruling (rendering rules, D1):** `יהוה` → **`Yahwe`**; `יה` → **`Yah`**. Refused in the
Name's seat: *Ṛebbi* · *Sidi Ṛebbi* · *Allah* · *Bab* · *Jehwa* · *Sidi* · *Dieu* ·
*Seigneur* · *LORD* · *Yahweh*. *Ṛebbi* is good Kabyle and stays lawful as a common
noun — it is not the Name.

**Theophoric compounds:** the compound follows **`Yahwe`** whole; the Name does not
bend to the compound (*Yahwa-*, *Yahu-* refused in the seat).

**Other divine names kept:** `אלהים` **Elohim** · `אל` **El** · `אלוה` **Eloha** ·
`אדני` (of God) **Adonai** · `שדי` **Caddai** · `יהוה צבאות` **Yahwe Ṣebaot**.

**Seen in the text:** `deuteronomy/6/4` — *Yisrael, cecleḍ: Yahwe Elohim-nneɣ, Yahwe d
yiwen.* Both `יהוה` rows carry `Yahwe`; `אחד` → *d yiwen*.

**Corrected at landing (2026-10-03):** a handful of Name seats came back mis-spelt
(*Yab*, *Yehweh*, *Yebbah*, bare *YHWH*) — clustered in 1 Kings 14:13–15, Numbers
3:13–16, 2 Samuel 22:14, 16, Genesis 24:7, Jeremiah 33:23–24 — or empty (Jeremiah 1:18),
or garbled (Psalm 145:14). All were re-rendered; the Name now reads `Yahwe` in every
seat.

## §2 · Lower-case gods; human lords

`אלהים` of idols and the gods of the nations → **`ṛebbi` / `iṛebbiten`**, lower case —
lawful, and distinct from **Elohim**. `exodus/32/4`: *Iṛebbiten-ik-agi, a Yisrael, i
k-issufɣen seg tmurt n Miṣrayim* (capital only because it opens the clause).
`אדני` · `אדון` of a human master → **bab**, **sidi**, **bab-iw**. Both senses are right.

## §3 · Words kept in Hebrew form (rendering rules, D2)

The Kabyle alphabet carries the Semitic letters: `ע` → **ɛ**, `ח` → **ḥ**, `צ` → **ṣ**,
`ק` → **q**, `ש` → **c** (/ʃ/), `ט` → **ṭ**.

| Hebrew | kept as | refused alone | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `רוח` | **ruaḥ**, every time | *ṛṛuḥ*, *aḍu* | one word, three senses — Spirit, wind, breath — the Hebrew leaves them one |
| `חסד` | **ḥesed** | *ṛṛeḥma* | |
| `צדק` · `צדקה` | **ṣedeq** | *lḥeqq* | |
| `תורה` | **Tora** | *ccariɛa*, *ccerɛ* | *ccariɛa* is the Islamic law's word |
| `שבת` | **Cabbat** | *ssebt* | a name |
| `משכן` | **Mickan** | *aqiḍun* | |
| `מנחה` | **minḥa** — not where it is a gift or tribute | | see §6 |
| `שאול` | **Ceol** | *jahennama*, *times* | not the Islamic hereafter |
| `משיח` | **Maciaḥ** — *win yettwadehnen*, of priest or king | *Lmasiḥ*, *Ɛisa* | Tanakh only |

Seen in the text: `genesis/1/2` *ruaḥ n Elohim*; `ezekiel/37/5` *ad d-awieɣ degwen ruaḥ, ad
tidirem* (`רוח` as breath, kept `ruaḥ`); `ezekiel/37/1` *s ruaḥ-is*.

## §4 · The prophet word — `navi`, never `nnbi`

**Ruling:** `נביא` → **navi**; `נבא` · `התנבא` → *ad yemmeslay d navi*. *Nnbi*, *lenbi*
and *ṛṛasul* are reserved titles of Muhammad; a reader must not meet them in Haggai.

**Jeremiah 23:13** · re-rendered 2026-10-03 · `הנבאו` (*they prophesied*, of Samaria's
prophets by Baal) had come back glossed *ad nnbi* — a verb built on the reserved
title. Re-rendered; the verse now reads *mmelsen d navi s Baɛal*.

## §5 · Fixed formulas and names (rendering rules, D3)

- `נאם יהוה` → **i d-yenna Yahwe**, never *lweḥy*. **Exception:** before a human
  name — `נאם בלעם` → **awal n Bilɛam**. Held at `numbers/24/3`.
- Personal and place names in Hebrew sounds, one spelling each: **Avraham**,
  **Yiṣḥaq**, **Yaɛqov**, **Moce**, **Aharon**, **David**, **Yisrael**, **Miṣrayim**,
  **Yerucalayim** — not the Arabic/Qurʾanic forms (*Ibrahim*, *Musa*, *Harun*,
  *Dawud*, *Lquds*).
- **Corrected at landing (2026-10-03):** *Musa* at Numbers 3:14, 3:16 and 1 Kings
  7:45; *lweḥy* at 2 Kings 19:25 and 1 Kings 16:20; *ccariɛa* at Ezra 7:14; *Dawed* at
  1 Chronicles 28:20; *Israyil* at Jeremiah 48:27 — all re-rendered.
  `numbers/3/14` now reads *Yahwe yemmeslay ⟨ɣer⟩ Moce deg tanefri n Sinayi*.

## §6 · Open flags — for a Kabyle ear

None of these is a ruling. Each waits for a native reader.

1. **`c` for `ש`** (Caddai · Cabbat · Ceol · Moce) — or *sh*? The text itself wavers on
   `שלמה`: mostly *Celomo*, but also *Shelomo* (e.g. 1 Kings 7:45) and *Selomo*. The
   rule is one spelling per name; which one is for a Kabyle ear.
2. **`navi` for `נביא`** — or a Kabyle word that is neither *nnbi* nor Islamic?
3. **`lɛahd` for `ברית`** — an Arabic loan; is there another?
4. **lower-case `ṛebbi`** for the gods of the nations — is it lawful Kabyle?
5. **`iqedcen`** (from the Hebrew root qdc) for `קדש` — is it understood?
6. **`ç` beside `č`.** The rules write `č`; older Kabyle orthography writes `ç`. Read, not
   corrected — a Kabyle reader decides.
7. **French accents inside Kabyle words** — *tesâiḍ*, *Yerâubben*, *lméahda*: likely `â`
   for `ɛ`, a glyph slip. Not corrected; for a Kabyle ear. (Nahum 3:14 is one.)
8. **ASCII `'` inside a word** (e.g. Joshua 8:15). Kabyle writes no such mark. Not
   corrected.
9. **`psalms/23/1` — `רעי` → *d amɣar-iw n wulli*.** *Amɣar* is *elder, old man*; the
   common Kabyle word for a shepherd is *ameksa*. Does *amɣar n wulli* carry *my
   shepherd*?
10. **`genesis/43/11` — `מנחה` → *am minḥa*.** The rule names this very verse as the
    place where `מנחה` is a *gift* to a man, not an offering, and so not *minḥa*.
    The verse does not yet follow its rule.
11. **`numbers/24/3` — `⟨את⟩` in the flow with no `את` in the Hebrew.** The flow reads
    *d awal n wergaz yellan ⟨את⟩ tiẓri-is teldi*; the token rows carry no marker,
    and `שתם העין` has none. A row/flow disagreement.
12. **`isaiah/53/5` — `נרפא לנו`.** The row for `נרפא` reads *i neḥla* and the row for
    `לנו` reads *neḥla* again: the same Kabyle word on two Hebrew tokens. The flow
    reads *d s tuḥda-s i neḥla*. An alignment question; `ובחברתו` (*by his stripes*) →
    *d s tuḥda-s* also wants a Kabyle ear.

## §7 · The re-check in progress (2026-10-06)

Verses whose token rows did not match the Hebrew token count, whose rows were empty,
or which carried **English words inside the supplied-word brackets** `⟨…⟩` were
removed and are being re-rendered now. Supplied words must be Kabyle; the English came
from examples in the rendering instructions that leaked into the brackets. Until the
re-rendering lands, those verses are absent from the repo. Entries here will record
what the re-rendering does not cure.
